My Steps for Starting a Business

  1. Come up with an idea you actually care about
  2. Do some research before you spend any money, such as:
    • Who is your customer?
    • Who are your competitors?
    • How much would people pay?
  3. Make a simple plan for your budget
  4. Start small and sell to a few real people
  5. Ask for feedback and improve
